\section{Symbol Glossary, Notation and Deterministic Subscript Key}

Use this index to quickly look up any variable, its physical meaning, and its standard unit.
To eliminate all ambiguity (such as distinguishing inductance from core length, conductor area from slot area, electrical loading, or moment of inertia), this formula collection consistently applies \textbf{deterministic subscripts}.

\subsection{Deterministic Subscripts and Index Key ($c$, $\mathrm{cond}$, $\mathrm{slot}$, $\mathrm{core}$, $g$, $t$, $y$, $\mathrm{ph}$, $\mathrm{LL}$, $m$, $f$, $s$, $r$, $b$, $\mathrm{iner}$)}

\begin{tabular}{@{}>{$}l<{$} p{7.5cm} >{$}l<{$}@{}}
\toprule
\text{Subscript} & \text{Meaning / Application} & \text{Examples}\\
\midrule

\mathrm{cond} & Single conductor & A_{\mathrm{cond}},\,J_{\mathrm{cond}},\,R_{\mathrm{cond}}\\
\mathrm{slot} & Stator or rotor slot & A_{\mathrm{slot}},\,b_s,\,h_s,\,\lambda_{\mathrm{slot}}\\
\mathrm{slot,cond} & Total conductor copper area in one slot & A_{\mathrm{slot,cond}} = z_s A_{\mathrm{cond}}\\
\mathrm{core} & Iron core cross-section and core loss & A_{\mathrm{core}},\,P_{\mathrm{core}},\,B_{\mathrm{core}}\\
g & Air gap & g,\,g',\,D_g,\,A_g,\,B_g,\,\Phi_g,\,F_g\\
t & Tooth & B_t,\,b_t,\,h_t\\
y & Yoke & B_y,\,b_y\\
\mathrm{ph} & Phase RMS value & V_{\mathrm{ph}},\,I_{\mathrm{ph}},\,E_{\mathrm{ph}},\,T_{\mathrm{ph}},\,R_{\mathrm{ph}}\\

m & Magnetizing branch & L_m,\,X_m,\,L_{aa,m},\,L_{ab,m}\\
f,\,F & Rotor field winding / excitation & N_f,\,I_f,\,U_f,\,R_f,\,E_F,\,P_{\mathrm{Cu,f}}\\
s & Stator, synchronous, or synchronizing & S_1,\,n_s,\,\omega_{ms},\,X_s,\,P_s,\,T_s\\
r & Rotor & S_2,\,R_2,\,L_{2\ell},\,\omega_2\\
\mathrm{st} & Starting / standstill condition ($s=1$) & T_{\mathrm{st}},\,I_{2,\mathrm{st}}\\
b & Breakdown / pull-out operating point & T_b,\,\omega_{2b}\\
\mathrm{iner} & Mechanical rotor inertia (moment of inertia) & J_{\mathrm{iner}}\\
\mathrm{mech},\,m & Mechanical speed / mechanical output & \omega_m,\,\omega_{ms},\,n_m,\,P_{\mathrm{mech}}\\
e & Electrical frequency ($2\pi f$) or electromagnetic & \omega_e,\,\omega_1,\,T_e\\
\mathrm{coil} & Single coil inductance and turns & L_{\mathrm{coil}},\,N_{\mathrm{coil}}\\

\mathrm{drop} & Fractional stator leakage voltage drop & \varepsilon_{\mathrm{drop}}\\
\mathrm{ecc} & Relative eccentricity ($e_0/g$) & \varepsilon_{\mathrm{ecc}}\\
