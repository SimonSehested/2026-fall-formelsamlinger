\subsection{Standard Subscripts, Operators, and Mathematical Conventions}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l p{9.0cm} p{5.5cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Notation} & \textbf{Meaning / Usage} & \textbf{Example / Context} \\
\midrule
$(\cdot)_{\mathrm{rms}}$ & Root-mean-square value over fundamental or switching period & $V_{\mathrm{rms}},\, I_s$ \\
$(\cdot)_{\mathrm{dc}},\, \overline{(\cdot)}$ & Average (DC) component over one period & $V_{\mathrm{dc}},\, I_o$ \\
$\hat{(\cdot)},\, (\cdot)_m$ & Peak amplitude of AC waveform & $\hat{V}_{\mathrm{control}},\, V_m$ \\
$(\cdot)_1$ & Fundamental frequency component ($50/60\text{ Hz}$ or $f_1$) & $I_{s1},\, V_{an1}$ \\
$(\cdot)_n,\, (\cdot)_h$ & $n$-th or $h$-th harmonic frequency component & $I_{sn},\, V_{LLn}$ \\
$(\cdot)_{LL}$ & Line-to-line value in three-phase systems & $V_{LL1},\, V_{LL,\mathrm{rms}}$ \\
$(\cdot)_{an},\, (\cdot)_{ph}$ & Phase-to-neutral (per-phase) value in three-phase systems & $V_{an1},\, I_{\mathrm{ph}}$ \\
$\Delta(\cdot)$ & Peak-to-peak ripple amplitude or parameter deviation & $\Delta i_L,\, \Delta V_o$ \\
$X, U, D$ & Upper-case letters: DC steady-state quiescent operating points & $X = -A^{-1}BU$ \\
$\hat{x}, \hat{u}, \hat{d}$ & Hat symbols: small-signal AC perturbations & $x(t) = X + \hat{x}(t)$ \\
$x, u, d$ & Lower-case symbols: total instantaneous quantities & $d(t) = D + \hat{d}(t)$ \\
CCM / DCM & Continuous / Discontinuous Inductor Conduction Mode & $i_L(t) > 0$ vs $i_L(t) = 0$ interval \\
SPWM & Sinusoidal Pulse-Width Modulation & Comparison of $v_{\mathrm{control}}$ with $v_{\mathrm{tri}}$ \\
RHP & Right-Half-Plane of Laplace $s$-plane ($\mathrm{Re}\{s\} > 0$) & Phase lag; non-minimum phase \\
PFC & Power Factor Correction & Active Boost ($I_s$ shaped to sine) \\
DAB / PSFB & Dual-Active-Bridge / Phase-Shifted Full-Bridge & Isolated bidirectional / bridge DC-DC \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
