\section{Zero-voltage switching quasi-resonant converters (ZVS-QRC)}

\subsection{Ctrl+F keywords: ZVS, zero-voltage, junction capacitor, \(C_j\), dual, true soft-switching, capacitor charging stage, \(\Phi=V/(ZI)\)}

ZCS is not fully soft: the device junction capacitor still dumps
\[
P_{C_j}=\frac12 C_{\mathrm{sw}} V_{\mathrm{sw}}^2 f_s
\]
at turn-on. ZVS places \(C_r\) across the switch so \(v_{\mathrm{SW}}\) rings through zero before turn-on.

ZVS switch: capacitor in parallel with the device. Half-wave: antiparallel diode clamps \(v_{Cr}\ge 0\). Full-wave: series diode allows \(v_{Cr}<0\). Full-wave ZVS is not used in practice: \(C_j\) energy cannot leave the die, so turn-on is still lossy.

ZCS and ZVS are dual: \(i_{Lr}\) and \(v_{Cr}\) waveforms swap roles. \(\Phi\) for ZVS is the reciprocal of the ZCS definition.

\subsection{ZVS-QR buck states}
Capacitor charging \([t_0,t_1]\): switch off, \(i_{Lr}=I_o\),
\[
v_{Cr}=\frac{I_o}{C_r}(t-t_0),\qquad T_{d1}=\frac{C_r V_{\mathrm{in}}}{I_o}.
\]
Ends when \(v_{Cr}=V_{\mathrm{in}}\) (\(v_{DF}=0\)).

Resonant \([t_1,t_2]\):
\[
v_{Cr}=V_{\mathrm{in}}+Z_0 I_o\sin\omega_0(t-t_1),\qquad
i_{Lr}=I_o\cos\omega_0(t-t_1).
\]
Ends at \(v_{Cr}=0\):
\[
T_{d2}=\frac{\alpha}{\omega_0},\qquad
\alpha=\sin^{-1}\Bigl(-\frac{V_{\mathrm{in}}}{Z_0 I_o}\Bigr)=\sin^{-1}(-\Phi).
\]
Half-wave: clamp at zero, then turn the transistor on. Full-wave: turn on while \(v_{Cr}<0\).
At \(t_2\): \(i_{Lr}=I_o\cos\alpha\).

Inductor recovering \([t_2,t_3]\):
\[
i_{Lr}=I_o\cos\alpha+\frac{V_{\mathrm{in}}}{L_r}(t-t_2),\qquad
T_{d3}=\frac{L_r I_o(1-\cos\alpha)}{V_{\mathrm{in}}}.
\]
Freewheel: \(T_{d4}=T_s-T_{d1}-T_{d2}-T_{d3}\).

\subsubsection{ZVS condition (buck)}
The AC swing must exceed the DC level:
\[
V_{\mathrm{in}}<Z_0 I_o \quad\Leftrightarrow\quad \Phi=\frac{V_{\mathrm{in}}}{Z_0 I_o}<1.
\]
Load current must be large enough (opposite of ZCS, which needs light enough current relative to \(V/Z\)).

\subsubsection{ZVS-QR buck conversion ratio}
\[
\frac{V_o}{V_{\mathrm{in}}}=1-g,\qquad
\Phi=\frac{V_{\mathrm{in}}}{Z_0 I_o}=\frac{R_n}{M}.
\]
\(0\le M\le 1\). \(M\) falls as \(f_s/f_0\) rises. Half-wave: load-dependent. Full-wave: almost load-independent.

\subsection{ZVS-QR boost}
Charging: \(T_{d1}=C_r V_o/I_{\mathrm{in}}\). Resonant:
\[
v_{Cr}=V_o+Z_0 I_{\mathrm{in}}\sin\omega_0(t-t_1),\qquad
i_{Lr}=I_{\mathrm{in}}\bigl(1-\cos\omega_0(t-t_1)\bigr).
\]
Recovering: \(T_{d3}=L_r I_{\mathrm{in}}(1-\cos\alpha)/V_o\). Gain:
\[
\frac{V_o}{V_{\mathrm{in}}}=\frac{1}{g}.
\]
Always \(M\ge 1\). \(R_n>M\) is required for ZVS.

\subsection{Normalised state durations (ZVS)}

\subsection{Ratings and practical notes}
Peak resonant voltage grows with \(I_o\) or \(I_{\mathrm{in}}\): high voltage rating is the main ZVS penalty. Not attractive for very high \(V_{\mathrm{in}}\). External \(C_r\) must be the desired \(C_r\) minus \(C_j\):
\[
C_{r,\mathrm{ext}}=C_{r,\mathrm{needed}}-C_j.
\]

\subsection{Exam recipe: ZVS-QR buck at the ZVS boundary}
\textbf{Use when:} given \(V_{\mathrm{in}}\), \(V_o\), \(f_s\), \(I_o\), \(C_j\); ``just able to achieve ZVS''.

\textbf{Steps:}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Boundary: \(Z_0=V_{\mathrm{in}}/I_o\).
  \item Full-wave / flat-\(F\) estimate: \(M=1-f_s/f_0\) \(\Rightarrow\) \(f_0=f_s/(1-M)=f_s V_{\mathrm{in}}/(V_{\mathrm{in}}-V_o)\).
  \item \(C_r=1/(\omega_0 Z_0)\), \(L_r=Z_0/\omega_0\) with \(\omega_0=2\pi f_0\).
  \item Subtract junction capacitance: \(C_{r,\mathrm{ext}}=C_r-C_j\).
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Fast checks:} If \(V_o=V_{\mathrm{in}}/2\) then \(f_0=2f_s\). Reducing \(I_o\) below \(V_{\mathrm{in}}/Z_0\) loses ZVS (simulation in the notes).

\textbf{Common traps:} using ZCS \(Z=V/I\) with \(I\) too small; forgetting to subtract \(C_j\); using \(M=f_s/f_0\) (that is ZCS buck, not ZVS buck).

\subsection{Recognition rules}
ZVS: voltage sinusoid through zero, current more rectangular/cosine. Dual of ZCS. Control still by \(f_s\).

\subsection{Common traps}
Full-wave ZVS does not eliminate \(C_j\) loss. ZVS needs heavy load; ZCS needs the resonant current amplitude \(\ge\) DC current. Mixing \(\Phi\) definitions between ZCS and ZVS.

\subsection{One-line checks}
ZVS buck \(M=1-g\) decreases with \(f_s\). ZVS boost \(M=1/g\) increases as \(g\) falls. Boundary \(Z_0 I = V\).
