\subsection{Exam strategy (open-book tests, closed-book final)}
Tests are open-book; the final is closed-book three-hour. If a clause in a multi-part statement fails (wrong topology, CCM vs DCM, ZCS vs ZVS \(\Phi\)), discard that option. Check limiting cases: \(D\to 0\), \(D\to 1\), \(\Phi\to 1\), \(R\to\infty\). Check inversion (buck-boost, Cuk, flyback polarity). Check units of \(Z_0\) (\(\Omega\)) and of \(K\) (dimensionless).
